# Explanation

Understanding-oriented. Nothing here tells you what to type. These pages explain why the repo is
shaped the way it is, so that when you need to change something structural you know what the shape
was protecting.

- **[sourcing-discipline.md](sourcing-discipline.md)** - the one rule, and what it costs to keep.
  Read this first; everything else is downstream of it.
- **[status-as-sole-discriminator.md](status-as-sole-discriminator.md)** - why there is no `kind`
  field, why maturity is a separate axis, and the store-only-what-you-cannot-calculate principle.
- **[one-file-per-entry.md](one-file-per-entry.md)** - why the data is 105 YAML files instead of one
  JSON array, and why ids are permanent.
- **[architecture.md](architecture.md)** - why there is no framework, why there *is* a build step,
  and why the build order is load-bearing.
- **[citation-rot.md](citation-rot.md)** - why a separate checker exists, and why silent failure is
  the worst kind.
- **[the-seo-layer.md](the-seo-layer.md)** - why a client-rendered app also ships hundreds of static
  pages.
